The shell

Everything a desktop environment gives you, drawn in one hand.

Bar, launcher, notifications, media dashboard, lock screen and a real settings app, made for Hyprland and built on Quickshell with a small Rust daemon behind it.

01 Launcher

Apps, actions, emoji and maths in one field.

No prefixes to remember. Apps are ranked by how often and how recently you open them, anything that looks like an expression is calculated, and > jumps to your projects.

SuperSpace

How the launcher works

02 Dashboard

Everything you glance at, under the clock.

Click the clock for an album-art player that follows any MPRIS app, your Google Calendar, Open-Meteo weather and a live graph of CPU, memory and disk.

Inside the dashboard

03 Appearance Studio

Browse a theme and the whole desktop tries it on.

Each card is a small desktop drawn in its palette. Moving through the carousel recolours the running shell in place; one switch writes GTK, Qt, kitty, btop, Neovim, Hyprland, Zen Browser, Fastfetch and the login screen.

Themes and wallpapers

04 Lock screen

The same ink, even when you step away.

The wallpaper blurs behind a serif clock on every monitor. It survives a shell crash, and the same screen greets you at login through greetd.

SuperShiftL

Lock and login screen

Notifications, grouped by app.

Toasts carry their actions; history and Do Not Disturb survive a restart. More

A clipboard that forgets secrets.

Images and text are recorded over Wayland; anything a password manager marks as secret never touches the disk. More

One palette, everywhere

Switch once. Every app follows.

The backend writes every application palette in one transaction, so nothing is ever left half-switched. Drag across the terminals.

05 Settings

A real settings app, in a real window.

Monitors, windows, input, the bar, default apps and the system, without editing a config file. It never touches your own Hyprland files: its overrides live in generated modules of their own.

Every settings page

Ten palettes

Pick one. This page wears it too.

Every palette is a short theme.conf paired with a painting. Choose one below and the site, its wallpaper and the screenshot change with it.

Screensaver

Scenes drawn in ASCII, chosen by the season.

Fragment shaders turned into characters, in your theme's colours or the season's. The previews run about three times faster than real time.

All nine scenes and the seasonal calendar

Install

From clone to desktop in one script.

The setup checks every prerequisite and stops before touching the system if anything is missing. --dry-run shows the plan first, and scripts/uninstall takes it all out again.
